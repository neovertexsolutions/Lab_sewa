Core Technology

Built with Enterprise Rigor. Designed for Speed.

Every tool inside MediFlow is crafted around the day-to-day pressure points of healthcare workers, eliminating double entries and automating repetitive administration.

📁

Integrated EMR & Patient History

Maintain longitudinal health records across consultations, vitals charts, lab findings, and pharmacy dispensations in one tamper-evident timeline.

  • Comprehensive allergy & chronic illness tags
  • Radiology & imaging attachments
  • Patient portal access with download controls
⚡

Smart Queue & Appointment Hub

Keep your waiting rooms calm with real-time patient queue trackers, SMS status notifications, and easy doctor schedule shifting.

  • Walk-in token generation
  • Online self-service booking widget
  • Automated cancellation & rescheduling slots
💊

Intelligent Pharmacy POS & Inventory

Eliminate medication expired write-offs with First-Expiry First-Out (FEFO) dispensing logic, automated reorder points, and quick POS billing.

  • Barcode scanner integration
  • Near-expiry batch highlighting
  • Supplier purchase order tracking
🔬

Laboratory Information System (LIS)

Complete pathology test management with standard medical templates, specimen barcodes, equipment interfacing, and instant PDF reports.

  • Custom reference ranges by age & sex
  • Digital pathologist approval signature
  • WhatsApp & SMS report dispatch
💳

Multi-Tier Billing & Tax Compliance

Collect payments faster with integrated invoice splitting, co-payments, insurance claims verification, and full tax invoicing.

  • Cash, Card, QR, and Online payments
  • Patient advance deposit ledgers
  • Daily revenue and cashier closing reconciliation
🛡️

Role-Based Security & Compliance

Ensure uncompromising data protection with granular staff access controls, complete change audit trails, and bank-grade data encryption.

  • HIPAA compliant architecture
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • Automated encrypted cloud backups
