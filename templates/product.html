DASHBOARD
01 — OVERVIEW

Your entire lab, at a glance.

The LabSewa dashboard shows you the day's activity instantly — how many patients walked in, how many samples were collected, how many tests ran, and how much revenue came in. No registers to flip through, everything sits on one screen.

  • Live numbers for patients, samples, tests, and revenue
  • Breakdown chart by test category
  • Today's appointments and recent activity in one place
View the dashboard →
Sample Registration
02 — SAMPLE ENTRY

Registering a sample now takes two minutes.

Patient details, sample type, collection time, and the test list — all in a single form. Fewer duplicate entries, fewer mistakes, faster turnaround.

  • Fill patient info and referring doctor together
  • Track sample type, collection date-time, and condition
  • Select tests and link them straight to the sample
Register a sample →
Patients
03 — PATIENT RECORDS

Every patient's full record, always searchable.

Find any patient instantly by name, phone, or ID. Visit history, gender, age, and contact details stay organized and searchable, so no old report ever needs to be redone.

  • Instant search by name, phone, or patient ID
  • Filter by gender and age group
  • Track each patient's last visit and total visits
View patients →
Test Management
04 — TEST CATALOG

Every test, panel, and TAT rule, defined once.

Build your full test catalog with pricing, sample requirements, and normal ranges, then group tests into panels for common profiles. Turnaround-time rules flag anything running late before a patient has to ask.

  • Individual tests and bundled panels with set pricing
  • Reference ranges by age and gender for accurate flags
  • Turnaround-time rules with automatic delay alerts
Manage tests →
Results
05 — RESULT ENTRY

From raw values to a validated result, in one flow.

Enter or import results against each ordered test, let out-of-range values flag themselves, and route every report through pathologist review before it's released. Nothing goes out unsigned.

  • Manual entry or analyzer import, with unit checks
  • Automatic flagging of abnormal and critical values
  • Pathologist review and e-signature before release
Review results →
Reports
06 — REPORT DELIVERY

Branded reports, delivered the way patients expect.

Every report carries your lab's letterhead, logo, and pathologist signature automatically. Generate a clean PDF the moment results are validated, and share it by print, email, or WhatsApp without retyping a thing.

  • Auto-branded PDF reports with digital signatures
  • One click to print, email, or send on WhatsApp
  • Full report history stored against each patient
See a sample report →
Billing & Payment
07 — BILLING

Invoicing and collections that reconcile themselves.

Generate an itemized invoice the moment tests are ordered, apply discounts or insurance coverage where they apply, and accept cash, card, or UPI at the counter. Every rupee is traceable back to a patient and a visit.

  • Itemized invoices with discounts and insurance splits
  • Cash, card, and UPI payments logged in real time
  • Daily collection and outstanding-dues reports
Manage billing →
Inventory
08 — INVENTORY

Reagents and consumables that never run out mid-test.

Track stock levels for every reagent, kit, and consumable across every branch. LabSewa raises a reorder alert before a low-stock item can stall the day's testing.

  • Live stock counts by branch and item batch
  • Automatic low-stock and expiry alerts
  • Purchase orders tracked from request to delivery
View inventory →
Appointments
09 — SCHEDULING

Walk-ins, bookings, and home collection on one calendar.

Patients book a slot online or over the phone, and phlebotomists see their home-collection route for the day laid out in order. No double-booked slots, no missed pickups.

  • Online and phone booking into a single calendar
  • Home-collection routes assigned to phlebotomists
  • Automatic reminders by SMS and WhatsApp
Book an appointment →
Staff Management
10 — STAFF & ROLES

The right access for every role, nothing more.

Technicians, pathologists, front-desk staff, and branch admins each get a login scoped to what their job needs. Shift rosters and activity logs keep every branch accountable.

  • Role-based permissions for every staff type
  • Shift rosters across single or multiple branches
  • Full activity log for every login and action
