Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade from Starter to Professional at any time. Your billing will simply prorate the difference automatically.

Is our patient medical data securely stored?

Yes. All patient records, diagnoses, and lab results are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and transmitted over TLS 1.3 with automated daily backups.

Do you help us migrate data from our old software?

Absolutely. Our onboarding team provides full data migration for patient demographics, drug catalogs, and historical lab test records at no extra cost for annual plans.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept bank transfers, credit/debit cards, eSewa, Khalti, and corporate invoicing with official tax VAT receipts.
