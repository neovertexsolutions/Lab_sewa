Security & Compliance

HIPAA Compliance

Protecting Healthcare Data With Security at the Core

Healthcare organizations trust us with sensitive information, so security and privacy are fundamental to how we design our platform. Our platform is built with security-focused features and practices intended to help healthcare organizations meet applicable privacy and security requirements.

Security-Focused Architecture

We implement comprehensive technical and physical safeguards such as:

🛡️
Role-Based Access Control Restrict patient data visibility based on medical staff credentials.
🔐
Secure User Authentication Multi-factor authentication (MFA) and encrypted session management.
⚡
Data Encryption in Transit TLS 1.3 protocol encryption across all API and browser transmissions.
💾
Protected Data Storage AES-256 bit encryption at rest for databases and digital medical files.
👥
Access Permissions & User Management Instant user provisioning and immediate offboarding revocation.
📋
Audit & Activity Tracking Immutable logs detailing who viewed, created, or modified any health record.
🏢
Secure System Infrastructure Tier-IV high-availability cloud hosting with automatic failovers.
🔍
Regular Security Monitoring & Updates Automated vulnerability patching and continuous threat monitoring.

Protecting Sensitive Healthcare Information

Healthcare organizations remain responsible for ensuring that their use of the platform complies with applicable laws and regulations.

We provide technical and administrative capabilities designed to support responsible handling of sensitive healthcare information and controlled access to authorized users.

Business Associate Agreements (BAA)

Where HIPAA applies and our services qualify as a Business Associate, appropriate contractual arrangements, including a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), should be established before applicable protected health information is processed.

To request our standard BAA or review enterprise security addendums, please email compliance@mediflow.com.

Our Commitment

We continuously work to improve our security practices, infrastructure, and privacy controls as healthcare technology and regulatory requirements evolve.

“Security. Privacy. Trust. Built into Healthcare Technology.”

⚠️
Important Notice:

HIPAA compliance depends on the complete environment—including your organization’s policies, configuration, workforce practices, vendors, and use of the platform—not simply the software itself.
